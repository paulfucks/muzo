How to Install as an App:
🍏 iPhone & iPad (iOS):

1. Open the link above in Safari.

2. Tap the Share icon (the square with the arrow pointing up) at the bottom.

3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" — Muzo installs as a native app with full offline controls!

🤖 Android (Samsung, Pixel, OnePlus, etc.):

1. Open the link in Chrome or your browser.

2. Tap the 3 dots menu and tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

🌐 Away from Home / Cellular (4G/5G):

On your home Wi-Fi, the link connects directly. To access Muzo when you're away from home, you can use a free Cloudflare Tunnel with 1 command for a worldwide HTTPS link!
